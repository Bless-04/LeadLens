Before You Start

📍

Find Your Water Service Line

Look where the main water pipe enters your home — usually in the basement, crawl space, or utility closet near the water meter.

🔦

Good Lighting

Make sure the area is well-lit. We'll need to see the pipe's color and texture clearly.

🧤

Safety First

Avoid scratching or disturbing old pipes. LeadLens uses non-invasive phone sensors only.

📱

How It Works

  1. Tap Test — Firmly tap your phone against the pipe to check if it's magnetic (steel).
  2. Camera Scan — If non-magnetic, snap a photo for color analysis (lead vs. copper).
  3. Instant Result — Get your pipe material and submit to the city.
